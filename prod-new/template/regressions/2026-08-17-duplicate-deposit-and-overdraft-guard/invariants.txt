This fixture's replay is checked against domain.ConservationHolds at EVERY
step (not only at the end), plus a final-balance assertion. Both invariants
this template ratifies are exercised:

  units_conserved
    Checked at every step via domain.ConservationHolds(before, event, after)
    -- an independent recomputation of what the after-state SHOULD be, not
    a tautological re-check of Apply's own output. Also checked once at the
    end: final balance must equal fixture.yaml's expect_final_balance
    (12.00000000 = 10 - 3 + 5, the two admitted deposits minus the one
    admitted withdrawal; the overdraft attempt at step 4 is rejected and
    contributes nothing).

  duplicate_event_single_effect
    Step 3 resubmits event ID "d1" (already applied at step 1) with the
    SAME amount. The harness asserts this step's effect is
    EffectDuplicateIgnored and that Balance/Version are unchanged from
    step 2's result.

See fixture.yaml's `# counterexample:` for the mutation that turns this
fixture red, and regressions/README.md for the empirical verification log.
